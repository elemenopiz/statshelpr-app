The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Quiz Content You Ask Us to Solve

When you click the extension's solve button on a Canvas quiz question, we send that question's text, its answer choices or fill-in-the-blank options, and any images attached to it — the page's "website content," in Chrome Web Store's data-disclosure terms — to our servers at api.statshelpr.com/api/solve so our AI can generate an answer. This content is processed by our backend and by our upstream AI model provider, Google Gemini, in order to produce the response; for calculation questions, our servers also execute the generated R code on our own compute infrastructure (Google Cloud Run) to produce the numeric result. Request data may be logged on our side for security, abuse prevention, and operational debugging.

6. Quiz Images That Require Your Canvas Login

Some quiz images are only viewable while you're logged into Canvas. For those, the extension fetches the image using your existing Canvas browser session so it can be included in the request described above. That session is used only to load the image from Canvas's own servers — we do not receive, see, or store your Canvas login, password, or session cookie. Once fetched, the image itself is sent to our servers like any other quiz content.

7. Files You Upload

CSV, TSV, or plain-text data files you add through the extension's popup — "user-generated content" — are stored locally in your browser (up to 5MB per file and 8MB total) and automatically removed after 7 days. While a file is active, its full contents are sent to our servers along with your solve requests. Our AI model provider does not receive the file itself: the AI is given only a summary of it — the file's name, its row and column counts, and for each column its name and type plus either summary statistics (mean, median, standard deviation, minimum, maximum, and count of missing values) or a list of that column's most frequent values and how often each occurs. The full file contents are passed only to the R execution service (Google Cloud Run) that runs the generated R code for calculation questions. Uploaded files are used only to answer your questions.

8. Install Identifier

The first time you install the extension, it generates a random identifier — not derived from your name, email, or Canvas account — and stores it via Chrome's built-in sync storage, which may follow you across browsers signed into the same Google account. This identifier is sent as an X-Install-Id header on solve and telemetry requests so we can apply the free plan's daily solve limit per install, and enforce the one-active-device rule on paid licenses described in item 9 below and in Section 7 of our Terms. If you start an upgrade from the extension, the identifier is also attached to your Lemon Squeezy checkout as order metadata so your license can activate on this install automatically after payment.

9. License Key (Paid Plans)

If you purchase Unlimited, checkout hands your Lemon Squeezy license key to the extension, which stores it via Chrome's sync storage. The key is sent as an authorization header on solve requests, and included in requests to activate a device, validate the license, or reset it so it can move to a new device — all needed to confirm your paid access and enforce the single-device limit described in Section 7 of our Terms.
