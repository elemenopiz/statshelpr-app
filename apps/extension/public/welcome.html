Welcome to statshelpr.

A tiny tutor that lives inside Canvas. Click one button per question and we'll work out the answer — concept questions or R-code calculations alike — and select it for you.

Nothing to configure — click the statshelpr icon in your Chrome toolbar (pin it from the puzzle-piece menu if you don't see it) and you're ready to go on any Canvas quiz page.

Built on the same R toolkit your course uses: tidyverse, mosaic, moderndive, ggplot.

Upgrading to Unlimited later? Checkout on statshelpr.com auto-activates your license the moment payment goes through — nothing to paste back into the extension.

step 4 of 5

For questions that need data files.

Some questions reference a CSV (e.g., "Using the houses dataset, compute…"). Drop your data files into the small panel in the bottom-right of the Canvas page — once per course, not once per question.
